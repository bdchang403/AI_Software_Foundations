# ADR-001: Exstream Branding Standards

## Context
All generated templates must strictly adhere to Corporate Marketing standards. 

## Decision
1. **Typography:** All headers must be Arial 14pt. Body text must be Arial 11pt.
2. **Color Palette:** 
   * Primary Header Color: `#003366`
   * Alert Text Color: `#FF0000`
3. **AI Constraint:** Do NOT let the AI invent or suggest any other colors or fonts outside of this approved list.
